The Beautiful Saint Martin

Saint Martin's Island, located in the Bay of Bengal, is Bangladesh's only coral reef island and southernmost point, known locally as Narikel Jinjira (Coconut Island) or Daruchini Dwip. Covering about \(8\text{\ km}^{2}\), this small island is famous for crystal-clear blue water, coconut trees, and vibrant marine life. Due to fragile ecosystems, tourism is restricted by the government to select months.
